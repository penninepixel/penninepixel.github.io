Privacy policy

Grid Harmony: Sudoku, by Pennine Pixel. Last updated 16 November 2026.

The short version

The app collects nothing about you. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no internet access of its own.

Buying the full version

The app is free to download. The full version is a one-off purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the whole payment, under Google's privacy policy; we never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the full version has been bought, and keeps a note on your phone so it works offline.

What the app stores

Your progress (the sudokus you have solved, your flock, wool, outfits, the sudokus you make, and your settings) is saved on your phone only. Nobody else can see it, including us.

Backups

  • If you have Android's own backup switched on, Android may include the app's saved progress in your Google account backup, under Google's privacy policy. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.
  • If you choose to make a backup file from Options, it is saved wherever you pick. It stays under your control.

Sharing

When you share a sudoku code, a postcard of your Fold or a solve, the app hands it to the app you choose (messages, email and so on). Nothing is sent anywhere else. Postcards are also saved to your phone's Pictures folder.

Links

The About screen has links to outside pages (such as Google Play, a support page or Discord). They open in your browser or that app, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. It uses Google Play's billing permission so that the full version can be bought.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
